Staff review checklist

Check these items before confirming a final price.

1. Confirm what the service includes

Driver + Van Only means no carrying assistance. Confirm the number of movers, packing hours and materials.

2. Verify access and lift conditions

Confirm collection and delivery floors, whether lifts work, parking/loading restrictions and distance.

3. Review special items and insurance

For pianos, antiques, fine art and large appliances, request details and determine whether specialist movers or additional cover are needed. Do not automatically confirm the price.

4. Confirm date, capacity and final quote

Check van and team availability manually. Adjust the quotation in the enquiry detail and move status to Confirmed only when ready.
